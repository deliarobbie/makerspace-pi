Traffic Light Pin Connections
Refer to docs/gpio_pin_map.png for physical pin locations.

Connection Mapping:
------------------------------------------------------
Traffic Light   | BCM GPIO   | Physical Pin Number
------------------------------------------------------
Red LED         | GPIO 8     | Pin 24
Yellow LED      | GPIO 7     | Pin 26
Green LED       | GPIO 25    | Pin 22
GND (Ground)    | Ground     | Pin 20 (or Pin 25)
------------------------------------------------------

Note:
The numbers in production/lights.py (LED(8), LED(7), LED(25))
use BCM GPIO numbering, matching the GPIO labels in the diagram.
